🐉 5D Dragon — a whole fantasy planet

You are a 5-dimensional dragon. The world, your body and the camera all live in R⁵ (x,y,z,w,v). What you see is the 3D slice through your position spanned by your right / up / forward axes. Turn into w and v and the same landscape morphs: mountains rise, oceans part, towers and dungeons appear and vanish.

Move ZS fwd/back · QD strafe · SpaceShift up/down · AE hidden-A ∓/± · WX hidden-B ∓/±  (physical keys, AZERTY labels shown)

Look mouse = yaw/pitch · ←→ roll · hold F+mouseX/mouseY/wheel = tilt slice into w-hidden (right-A / up-A / fwd-A) · hold G = same into v-hidden · hold F+G+mouseX = hidden-hidden spin · R re-align · T ghost slices · hold H dimension-sense · C camera · B home beacon · Esc settings

Seed in URL ?seed=…, position saved live in #hash — send the URL to a friend to share a place, including hidden w/v. Find the runes: they only glow near phase-locked secrets.
